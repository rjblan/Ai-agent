## Platform

web

## Stack

React + Vite (confirmed by the owner). Sonner for toasts and Motion for springs/gestures,
per the repo's `pick-ui-library` skill.

## Users

*Inferred.* Spanish-speaking individuals and small-team operators who juggle many small
digital chores (inbox triage, scheduling, reports, file cleanup) and want to hand them off
in plain language from their phone.

## Product Purpose

*Inferred.* Ai-agent is a personal AI agent: you describe a task in plain words, it plans
the steps, does the work across your tools, and asks before anything irreversible.
Success is the user delegating a chore and getting back a finished result plus a
readable record of what was done.

## Positioning

*Inferred.* The agent shows its work: every task becomes a visible plan with steps the
user can approve, pause, or undo, instead of a chat answer that hides what happened.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Mock site only: no backend, no real integrations, no sign-up.
- Copy is in Spanish.
- Must feel native on a phone (the owner works from mobile).

## Evidence on Hand

None. No customers, testimonials, pricing, benchmarks, or integrations exist; the site
must not invent them as facts. Demonstration data is allowed when labeled as an example.

## Product Principles

1. Show the plan before acting.
2. Ask before anything irreversible.
3. Every action leaves a readable record.
